# Pet Stayz — Going Live

## Market context
Pet Stayz targets the gap between global players like Rover.com and the needs of Australian pet owners who want better local discovery, suburb-first search, and a more premium booking UX.

## Why now
- Pet ownership in Australia remains high, with growing demand for flexible boarding, home sitting, and daycare.
- Existing marketplaces feel generic, especially on mobile web.
- Hosts need better conversion, clearer availability, and simpler payout tooling.

## Key live integrations
- **alphinium-payments**: real payment escrow, deposits, final capture, host payouts, refunds, and damage cover workflows.
- **alphinium-booking**: host availability calendar sync, blocked dates, instant-book rules, and stay management.
- **alphinium-maps**: GPS map browse, suburb clustering, and nearby host discovery.
- **Background checks**: third-party API for host identity verification and trust signals.
- **ChatInstance**: guided search, booking assistance, and lead capture for guest support.
